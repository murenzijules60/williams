❓ Frequently asked questions

Do you offer veterinary advice before purchase?
Yes! Our in-house veterinary team offers free guidance on product selection, dosage, and treatment plans. Contact us via phone or email.
How fast is delivery across Rwanda?
We deliver within 24–48 hours in Kigali, and 2–3 business days nationwide. Express options available for urgent orders.
Can I order animal drugs in bulk for my farm?
Absolutely. We provide wholesale pricing for livestock farms, cooperatives, and vet clinics. Request a quote via our contact form.
Are your products certified and safe?
All items are sourced from GMP-certified manufacturers and approved by Rwanda Agriculture and Animal Resources Board (RAB).
