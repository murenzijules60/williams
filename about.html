🐾 About Jules Animal Health

Dedicated to the well-being of every animal, from livestock to beloved pets. We combine veterinary expertise with compassionate care to deliver premium animal health solutions across East Africa and beyond.

Our mission

At Jules Animal Health, we believe that healthy animals create thriving communities. Founded in 2018 by veterinary pharmacist Jules Murenzi, our company was born from a vision: to provide farmers, veterinarians, and pet owners with reliable, safe, and affordable veterinary drugs & supplements. We bridge the gap between modern veterinary science and everyday animal care.

Today, we serve over 500+ livestock farms, veterinary clinics, and individual pet owners. Every product in our catalog is sourced from GMP-certified manufacturers and vetted by licensed professionals to ensure efficacy, safety, and compliance.
